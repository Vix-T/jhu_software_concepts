Vix Talbot

Data Scientist & MSAI Candidate, Johns Hopkins University

I'm a data scientist with a background in consulting, now pursuing my Master's in Artificial Intelligence at Johns Hopkins as I transition toward machine learning engineering. My work spans computational stylometry, healthcare research, and NLP.

Integrity and rigor guide how I work. During a neuroscience internship, my final presentation included data I'd carefully processed; a senior researcher in attendance recognized an inconsistency it revealed, which traced back to a flaw in the study's underlying protocol. Watching that come to light shaped how I think about transparency and reproducibility, and led me to interrogate a race as a proxy variable that surfaced later in my own thesis rather than let it stand unexamined. I care as much about how a model is built as what it predicts, and about bringing the people I collaborate with along with genuine care, not just technical competence.
